# A20. Participate in a discussion with your friends about cybersecurity event.

I started a discussion with two of my friends about the alleged Malaysia Airlines ransomware attack claimed by the Qilin ransomware group which happened in late February. I chose this topic as we are all Malaysian students who have flown on this airline before. I shared a news link about the incident, and we discussed the nature of the attack, who was responsible, and what it meant for us as previous passengers.

As it is recent, the airline had not officially confirmed the breach, Qilin listed the airline on their dark web leak site, potentially as a pressure tactic.

I directed the conversation to flow to similar attacks done by the same group on Kuala Lumpur International Airport in March 2025, which disrupted flight displays, check in counters, and baggage systems for over 10 hours, with a 10 million ransom refused by the prime minister.

Both my friends expressed concern about whether their data have been compromised, which led me to recommend Have I Been Pwned as a tool to check if our emails have appeared in any known data breaches.
