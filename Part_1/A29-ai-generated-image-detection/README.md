At first glance, the image looks convincing. However, with closer inspection, the image shows several AI artifacts. For instance:

- The signage tries to replicate Japanese characters but are mostly inconsistent characters. `refer to 1_sign`
- The background figures appear blurred and sometimes disfigured  `refer to 1_figure`
- The road layout does not make sense, and zebra crosses and wet spots are muddled together  `refer to 1_ground`
- Her coat collar merges with her dress  `refer to 1_collar`

These are common indicators of AI generated content, as they tend to struggle with text, and peripheral consistency. They lack “awareness” and “context clues”, so things might look out of place.

However, as AI image/video generation gets better, these artifacts will be harder to identify. As such, AI detection tools like Hive’s are essential as they are most likely trained on large datasets of such images and can analyse statistical patterns that are invisible to the human eye like the image noise, pixel distributions, lighting and texture inconsistencies.

This activity highlights the importance of AI detection tools, as photorealistic images like these can be mistaken for genuine photographs to unsuspecting individuals and be used to spread misinformation.
