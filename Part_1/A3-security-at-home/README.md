# A3. Discover security concepts used in your house.

**Overview:** I live at Trinity Residential College. I have identified the physical and digital security measures that are in place that protect residents, belongings and the premise.

**Security concepts found:**

1. **Perimeter Fencing:** Trinity is fully fenced around the entire residential campus. This restricts the public from simply walking in, making this the outermost layer of security. This preventive physical control establishes a clear boundary between the college and the surrounding area. Entrances to these gates require either RFID or a key.
2. **RFID Access Control:** RFID (fobs) access is used throughout the residential area, with access via specifically coded gates and wing buildings that can only be opened by residents of that wing. This creates two layers of RFID-controlled access, the first being placed at the perimeter gates, and the second across the wing buildings. This makes it much more secure as if someone tailgates through the main gate, they would still not be able to enter the buildings.
3. **24/7 Duty Officer and CCTV:** Trinity employs a Duty Officer every night (and day) of the year to contactable and to respond to any emergencies or student concerns. There are also 24/7 CCTV and on-site staff for peace of mind of residents.
4. **Room Locks and One-way balcony door:** Each resident room has its own lock, with a unique physical key that could only unlock it as the final personal layer of security to the room. Also, the balcony door has a one-way lock. This means it can be opened from the inside, but not the outside. This is a deliberate security design that prevents entry through the balcony. To further secure my belongings, I have also used locks on my wardrobe and put other belongings into luggage that are secured with (TSA approved) locks.

**Digital Security:**

For new students, Trinity currently uses Wi-Fi that is password protected, however, all residents share the same password, which is a relatively weak security model since a leaked password could compromise the network. On the digital side, residents access a StarRez online portal to manage accommodation contracts, submit forms and to look at available services.
